# Results overview

The raw 1,000-control family retains GABAA (BH q approximately 0.018) and
iGluR (approximately 0.042). Kv has positive held-out predictive performance
but lacks specificity against matched controls. NaV6 is nominally above many
controls, but does not pass six-test BH; NaV7 demonstrates gene-definition
sensitivity. Total library count has negative held-out R² in the reported
technical-target models.

Raw and residualized specificity are separate inferential families. A
prediction-to-internal-consistency ratio is contextual information, not a
formal ceiling. Matched controls account for expression and detection but
do not directly match gene co-expression. See the complete Supplement for
subclass, retuning, calibration, attribution, and gene-omission sensitivities.

The analysis supports predictive association and conditional molecular
specificity for selected targets. It does not establish causal regulation or
equivalence between mRNA measurements and membrane conductance.
